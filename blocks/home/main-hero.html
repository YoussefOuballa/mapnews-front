HM the King Performs Eid Al-Fitr Prayer in Rabat, Receives Greetings on Joyous Occasion

25 March 2026

Rabat - His Majesty King Mohammed VI, Commander of the Faithful, may God assist Him, accompanied by HRH Crown Prince Moulay El Hassan, HRH Prince Moulay Rachid and HH Prince Moulay Ahmed, performed on Friday the Eid Al-Fitr prayer at the "Ahl Fez" mosque in Mechouar in Rabat, and received greetings on this joyous occasion.
